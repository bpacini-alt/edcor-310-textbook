# Future Revision Ideas — Chapters 13–14

**Status: Deferred.** This is an idea archive, not an implementation plan or commitment to revise Chapters 13–14 now.

The current Chapters 13–14 should remain as they are while larger priorities take precedence. The ideas below preserve useful work from EDCOR pull requests #34, #53, and #59 without keeping those implementation branches active. Revisit them together in a future chapter revision.

## Editorial and interpretive ideas from PR #34

- Distinguish clearly between empirical findings, interpretive frameworks, and the author’s professional or faith-based perspective.
- Use frameworks as lenses and questions for thinking, not as diagnoses of individual students or classrooms.
- Tighten repeated explanations while preserving the major topics and examples.
- State a professional baseline around accurate terminology and student dignity.
- Preserve the law, district-policy, and context caveats rather than presenting classroom guidance as universally transferable.
- Keep the chapter’s application thoughtful and evidence-aware without making every claim sound more certain than the evidence allows.

## Practice ideas from PR #53

These are possible practice activities, not a finalized recurring practice system:

- Retrieval and explanation of key ideas.
- “Choose” tasks in which students select an appropriate instructional or interpretive move.
- “Judge” tasks in which students evaluate evidence, an explanation, or a proposed response.
- “Transfer” tasks that connect course ideas to a new classroom situation.
- Evidence-Based Practice Response prompts that ask students to identify evidence, reason from it, justify a response, and acknowledge limits.
- Student choice, optional test-out, and flexible practice should remain possible; none of this is a mandatory chapter routine.
