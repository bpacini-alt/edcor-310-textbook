# Precise Practice Chapter 16 usability iteration — October 31, 2026

Decisions piloted in both textbooks:

- Every concept checkpoint begins collapsed; expand only when the student chooses practice. Econ embeds use native details/summary; EDCOR already uses a collapsed panel.
- Add a low-stakes, per-tier *suggested* progress bar: Tier 1 **10**, Tier 2 **5**, Tier 3 **3** attempted checks. These are provisional experience-design values, **not measured fluency, mastery, or automatic promotion**. Students may move on sooner or practice longer. The main effort belongs in the chapter-end mixed test-out and constructed-response reasoning.
- Spiral-review chapter checkboxes are sorted by **Unit → Chapter** and all available prior banks start checked. Students may deselect chapters to narrow practice. Chapter 16 remains included; the final mixed readiness test still requires at least one earlier chapter, while concept checkpoints permit completely focused practice.
- Existing earlier-chapter banks are small prototype samples; the set of selectable chapters is not yet comprehensive or human-validated.
- Important design caveat: counting attempts can reward repetition rather than genuine coverage; experiment with distinct-question counting or quality indicators after usability feedback, without turning this into a mastery algorithm.
- Browser, iframe-height, state-persistence, accessibility, and classroom validation are still outstanding.
