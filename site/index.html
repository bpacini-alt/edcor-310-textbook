How the book is organized

I Unit 1 · Chapters 1–8 Intro to Psychology Every course has to start somewhere, and this one starts with you. Before you can evaluate a teaching strategy, a research claim, or a neuromyth, you have to reckon with the instrument doing the evaluating: your own mind. II Unit 2 · Chapters 9–14 Developmental Psychology Unit 1 gave you a way to weigh evidence and catch your own blind spots. Now point those tools at a moving target: the developing child. III Unit 3 · Chapters 15–20 Motivational Psychology Unit 2 explained how students grow. This unit explains why they try — or don't. IV Unit 4 · Chapters 21–26 Cognitive Psychology Units 1 through 3 covered why students believe what they believe, how they develop, and what drives them to engage. This final unit turns to the machinery underneath all of it: attention, memory, and cognitive load as tools you design around, not just concepts to memorize.

About this book

What began as a classroom project to enhance student learning in educational psychology and human development has grown over three years into a rigorous, collaborative textbook. This volume integrates student writing, generative AI assistance, and faculty oversight into a free educational resource.

Ultimately, the authors and editors maintain full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the content herein.
