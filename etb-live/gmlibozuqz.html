Unit Outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

Unit 2 explained how students grow. This unit explains why they try — or don't. You'll dig into agency and self-efficacy, the fragile difference between motivation that lasts and motivation that a reward quietly kills, and the pull of belonging that shapes whether a student shows up ready to learn or shows up ready to disappear.

Motivation research answers why a student engages, but it doesn't fully answer how their mind processes what happens next once they do. That mechanical question — attention, memory, load — is where the course closes, in Unit 4.
