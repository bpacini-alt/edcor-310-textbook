Unit Outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

Units 1 through 3 covered why students believe what they believe, how they develop, and what drives them to engage. This final unit turns to the machinery underneath all of it: attention, memory, and cognitive load as tools you design around, not just concepts to memorize. You'll pressure-test popular claims about intelligence and learning styles, and confront the gap between what research has settled and what actually reaches classrooms.

That gap is the note this course ends on deliberately. Everything in Units 1 through 3 is only useful to the extent it changes what you actually do in a classroom — which is the same test this unit, and this course, ultimately asks of you.
