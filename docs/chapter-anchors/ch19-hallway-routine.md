# Chapter 19: Preschool hallway routine

- **Book:** Joy of Learning
- **Chapter:** 19, “Extrinsic Motivation & Behaviorism”
- **Anchor:** A practiced routine helps a class move safely together.
- **Core idea:** Predictable cues and rehearsed actions shape behavior and reduce the need for constant teacher direction.
- **Scene mapping:** A teacher leads preschoolers along one side of a hallway. Most children hold a walking rope with the left hand and raise two fingers with the right; a few children and passersby show natural variation.
- **Misconception to avoid:** Routines are not rigid, automatic compliance. They are procedures teachers teach, practice, and reinforce so children know what to do.
- **Placement:** Immediately after the Chapter 19 title card, before the opening hook.
