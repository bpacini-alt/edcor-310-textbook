## How the scene carries the idea

The two flags and the matching pennants make group membership immediately legible: the boys have formed the Eagles and the Rattlers. The scene anchors the chapter’s account of group identity and the quick emergence of “us” and “them.” It does not imply that group identity alone inevitably produces hostility; the chapter ties the escalation to competition and later describes cooperation as a route back.

The blue and amber color coding is an illustration choice, not a claim about the historical groups’ colors. The historically grounded markers are the team names and the flags and shirts described in the chapter.
