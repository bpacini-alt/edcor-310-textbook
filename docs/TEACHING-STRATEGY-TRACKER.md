## Goal

Plan for **one practical teaching strategy in each chapter**. A second or third can be added when it teaches a meaningfully different move; the maximum is three per chapter.

A strategy is a concrete instructional move a teacher or teacher candidate can use in connection with the chapter’s learning science. It should make clear what the teacher does, what learners do, what learning goal it serves, and how the teacher can tell whether it helped. General principles, chapter aphorisms, and student study techniques are tracked elsewhere.

### Status guide

- **Unstarted:** No candidate has been drafted.
- **Candidate:** A strategy has been described, but it has not been reviewed.
- **Needs review:** Benjamin flagged a wording, evidence, fit, or implementation question.
- **Approved:** Benjamin approved the exact strategy; it is not yet counted as placed.
- **Placed:** The approved strategy is in a merged chapter change.
- **Verified:** The live page was checked after merge.
- **Exception:** The chapter has a documented reason to remain below one strategy.

Keep the chapter ledger’s placed count synchronized with the entry log. Optional strategies need their own learner action and a distinct instructional purpose; small variations of one activity do not count as separate strategies.
